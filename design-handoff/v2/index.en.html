Thesis

Three convictions that hold up what I publish.

01

Built from real pain, not to sell more.

Technology should exist to solve something for someone. I've been saying this since a talk at TVN Panama in 2019, years before generative AI was fashionable. It isn't opportunistic narrative.

02

LATAM can be a power on its own terms.

Our real edge isn't building the models, that fight belongs to another league. It's knowing our own problems firsthand. The model is a commodity. The terrain is not.

03

Solving over selling.

I filter by values, not by company size. If the brief works against people, I'm not the right person. And yes, my experience has a price: charging fairly for doing the work well is coherence with what I preach, not greed.
