I bring to the stage the same clarity I apply with CEOs: I translate artificial intelligence into the language of business, without hype, without abstractions, with enough technical depth that it never feels shallow. I make something difficult easy to understand, and useful the following Monday.

Why bring me in

AI + business + LATAM, in human language.

I am not the technician who complicates the conversation, nor the evangelist who sells smoke. I am the one who translates: I explain how AI changes your company's decisions, what of the new matters and what is noise, and why LATAM has an advantage almost no one is naming. When I finish, the room understands, and leaves with something it can apply.

Topics

Three topics, deep but accessible.

01

AI Governance

How to adopt artificial intelligence without losing control. Real risks, how to decide what to build vs. buy, what to demand from your providers, and why data protection is not enough. For boards and technology committees.

02

Corporate responsibility with AI

AI is accelerating decisions that used to take months. How to navigate its impact on jobs, customers and community, without falling into panic or window-dressing. An uncomfortable conversation, but a necessary one, for leaders who want to respond well.

03

AI basics for the C-level

What a CEO or board member needs to understand about AI in a 60-minute session: what a language model is, what an agent is, what a context window is, where the money is, and how to evaluate an AI proposal without being technical. Zero unnecessary jargon.

Frequently asked questions

About booking me to speak.

How do I book you?

Write to me at yo@camilo-ramirez.com with the context of the event: type, audience, date, city or virtual, language, and budget if you have it clear. I reply personally with availability and an indicative fee.

What language can you speak in?

Native Spanish (neutral Colombian) and professional English (C1). On stage in either one without friction.

In person or virtual?

Both. For in person I fly from Bogotá. For virtual I have my own professional setup (camera, audio, lighting). I do not improvise from a laptop.

What topics do you speak on?

AI governance, corporate responsibility with AI, and AI basics for the C-level. The common thread: translating artificial intelligence into the language of business, without hype and without abstractions.

Do you implement AI in my company?

No, implementation is done by Navigamo, the company I co-founded. I am a speaker and advisor; executing is my team's work. If your event aims to activate your organization with AI, the talk plus the handoff to Navigamo is the natural flow.

Do you accept pro bono talks?

Exceptionally, aligned with specific causes. It is not the rule. My time and experience have a price. If your organization is nonprofit and the cause fits, write to me and we talk.

Do you have stage material (photo, video, sizzle reel)?

There is recent stage material on my LinkedIn and YouTube.
